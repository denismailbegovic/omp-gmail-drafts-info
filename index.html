OMP Gmail Drafts

A private, locally operated desktop connector that lets its operator use OMP to search and read individually authorized Gmail accounts and create unsent email drafts.

What the connector does

  • Searches and reads messages on request, without automatically downloading entire mailboxes.
  • Creates new-message drafts or reply drafts attached to an existing Gmail conversation, for human review.
  • Does not expose operations for sending, deleting, trashing, forwarding rules, or changing mailbox labels.

Google’s gmail.compose authorization also permits sending and managing drafts. The connector imposes narrower application-level restrictions; these are not a Google-enforced draft-only permission. It also requests gmail.readonly to read authorized messages.

Private operation

This website is informational only. It has no sign-in form, mailbox interface, analytics, or connector service. It does not collect email messages or OAuth credentials. Authorizations are performed separately for each mailbox by the local application; listing multiple accounts does not share their mailboxes with each other.

Retrieved email content may be processed by the AI provider configured in OMP. Review the privacy notice and the provider’s policies before using the connector with sensitive mail.
